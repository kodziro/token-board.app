Token Board FAQ

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Short answers to the most common questions about token boards and about Token Board, the free online token board at token-board.app.

What is a token board?

A token board is a visual reward chart: a child earns a token for each success and trades a full board for a reward chosen in advance. It comes from ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis) and is used in therapy, in classrooms and at home.

How is a token board used in ABA therapy?

In ABA, a token board is used to reinforce a target behavior. The therapist picks the reward with the child first, gives a token right after each target behavior, and exchanges the full board for the reward. The guide ABA Token Board Online shows the steps.

How many tokens should I start with?

Start with 3 to 5 tokens for a beginner, and 10 for most kids who already know the system. The first reward should come quickly, then you can raise the goal. The guide How to Use a Token Board shows the steps.

Can I use it in a classroom?

Yes. The online board runs on an interactive whiteboard, a projector or a Chromebook, with nothing to install. Use one board for the whole class for a shared routine, or one board per student for an individual goal.

Does it work in teletherapy?

Yes. Share the browser tab or window in Zoom or Google Meet, tap the slots on your side, and the child sees each token appear. Turn on "share sound" in the sharing dialog so the child also hears the tone and the celebration.

Is there an app for iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Token Board: ABA Reward Chart runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac and is free to download in the App Store. There is no Android version; on other devices, use the online board in the browser.

Is it free?

Yes. The online token board is free, with no account and no ads: a token goal of 3, 5 or 10 with a star token, a white background and a gift reward. Other token goals, icons, backgrounds, rewards and your own photos are extras: an optional one-time unlock for $19.99 plus tax where applicable adds them all, with no subscription. The free board keeps working without it.

Is the web unlock the same as the app's premium?

No. The unlock is for the web version at token-board.app only. The app "Token Board: ABA Reward Chart" for iPhone, iPad and Mac is free to download in the App Store; its optional premium, Token Board Plus, is bought separately there.

Is anything uploaded?

Everything you set, including photos, is saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts and no ad tracking; anonymous usage statistics only. If you buy the one-time unlock, we store the email address you paid with, your license key and the payment id, so we can deliver and restore your purchase; your board and photos still stay in this browser.

Does a token board replace advice from a professional?

No. Token Board is a support tool, not a substitute for professional advice from a BCBA or clinician.
