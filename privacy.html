Privacy Policy – token-board.app

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what token-board.app does with your data. The short version: your board, its title and your photos stay in your browser and are never sent to us, and for a purchase we store only what is needed to deliver and restore your license.

  • Who we are. Apps for Parents (Natalia Jakubczyk-Gajewska), Poland. Contact: support@token-board.app. This page covers the website and the browser unlock. The app "Token Board: ABA Reward Chart" for iPhone, iPad and Mac has its own privacy policy.
  • The free tool. Everything you set, including photos, is saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts and no ads.
  • Anonymous usage statistics. We use TelemetryDeck to count how the site is used. The data is anonymous and aggregated: no personal data and no tracking across sites. It tells us how the board is used (for example that a board was started or completed, which kind of setting was changed and how long a visit lasted), which links to the App Store and to our other sites are clicked, and whether the unlock is used (opened, bought, restored or failed). The board title, your photos, your license key and your email address are never part of these statistics.
  • Buying the unlock. You pay on Stripe's page; we never see your card details. Link (Stripe) is the seller and has its own privacy policy. From Stripe we receive and store the email address you paid with, your license key and the Stripe ids of the payment, together with the state of the license (paid, refunded or disputed) and its dates. We use them to deliver, restore and support your purchase and to handle refunds. Your license key is also saved with the payment at Stripe, so that a purchase can be found by its key. Our records are stored on Cloudflare, which hosts our unlock service; the emails with your key are sent through Resend. Our providers may process data outside the EU under EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We keep the records for the life of the license. On request we delete your email address — after that you can still restore with your license key or restore link, but not by email. Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract) for the purchase data, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest) for abuse checks and rate limiting.
  • Restoring by email. When you type an email address into the Restore field, it is sent to our unlock service to look for a purchase. If that address paid, we send the key and the restore link to it. An address that did not pay is not stored.
  • In your browser. The unlock keeps your license key and a random device id in your browser's localStorage. They are needed for the unlock to work. The random id only lets us count the devices per key (an abuse check); it identifies no person, it is never part of the usage statistics, and we delete each device record 30 days after it was last seen. Clearing your site data removes them; your restore link brings the key back. The free online token board also saves, in your browser only: your board (its title, the tokens earned, the token goal, the token icon, the background, the reward and the sound setting); your own photos, made smaller on your device (the original file is never stored); and a few small notes for the page itself — how often and since when this browser has opened the board, how often the Style panel was opened, which hints were already shown and when you last answered the feedback question. None of this is sent to us.
  • Rate limiting. Cloudflare processes your IP address to protect the service from too many requests. We do not store it.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, it processes your IP address to deliver the pages.
  • Email. When you write to us, we use your message only to answer you. We do not sell or share personal data.
  • Your rights. You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, or object to its use — write to the contact address above. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority (PUODO).
