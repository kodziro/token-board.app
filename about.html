About Token Board

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Token Board is a free online token board that runs in the browser, at token-board.app. A child earns a token for each success and trades a full board for a reward chosen in advance. It is made for ABA therapists, teachers and parents.

Who makes it

Apps for Parents is the maker of Token Board. Apps for Parents was founded by Natalia Jakubczyk-Gajewska, who also writes the guides on this site.

What we make

The online token board on this site is free, with no account and no ads. The same maker's app, Token Board: ABA Reward Chart, runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac and is free to download in the App Store. There is no Android version of the app; the online board works in any browser.

How we work

Nothing you put on the board is uploaded: the board, its title and your photos stay in your browser. See the FAQ for what we store if you buy the web unlock. The guides name their sources, and each page shows when it was last updated.
