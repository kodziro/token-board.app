Terms of Use – token-board.app

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. What this covers

These terms cover the website token-board.app, the free online token board on it, and the one-time unlock for the online token board in your browser. The app "Token Board: ABA Reward Chart" for iPhone, iPad and Mac is sold separately in the App Store, under Apple's terms and Apple's standard license agreement.

2. The free tool

The online token board is free. It needs no account and shows no ads. Nothing is uploaded: the board, its title, settings and photos stay in the browser.

The tool is provided as is: we do our best to keep it working, but we cannot promise it will always be available or free of errors.

The online token board is a support tool, not a substitute for professional advice from a BCBA or clinician.

3. Buying the unlock

When you buy the one-time unlock, two separate contracts apply.

  1. The purchase contract is with Link (Stripe). The purchase is "Sold through Link": Link, Stripe's Managed Payments service, is the seller and merchant of record. It takes the payment, charges any tax, issues the receipt and handles refunds and disputes under its own buyer terms. Any statutory right to withdraw from a purchase of digital content (EU right of withdrawal) is governed by those terms. We never see your card details. The charge appears on your statement as LINK.COM* APPS4PARENTS.
  2. The license for the content is from Apps for Parents (Natalia Jakubczyk-Gajewska), Poland. It unlocks the extras of the online token board in your browser: your own photos, up to 20 boards, any token goal from 3 to 100, and all 59 rewards, 23 token icons and 8 backgrounds. It is a lifetime license for one payment, with no subscription and no renewal. It is for the devices you use yourself — as many as you like. Sharing the key with others is not allowed. If a key is shared with others, we may cancel it. Everything in the free online token board stays free.

The price is $19.99 plus tax where applicable — the exact total is shown before you pay. The price is in US dollars. The unlock is for the web version only; the iPhone, iPad and Mac app "Token Board: ABA Reward Chart" is sold separately in the App Store.

4. Your license key

After payment you see your license key on the page, and we send it to you by email. The unlock is saved in your browser. You can restore it on your devices with the restore link, the license key or the email address you paid with. Safari may forget the unlock after a week; shared or managed computers may clear it when you sign out — then open the restore link again. Keep your key private. If you lose it, restore by email or write to us.

5. Refunds

On top of the buyer terms of Link (Stripe), we give a voluntary refund: write to us within 14 days of the purchase and we ask Stripe to refund you in full, no questions asked. After a refund or a chargeback the license is cancelled and the key can no longer be used or restored.

6. Availability and changes

"Lifetime" means the lifetime of the service (this website and the online tool on it), not a fixed number of years. We may change, pause or end the sale of the unlock. Keys you have already bought keep working for as long as we run the service. We may update these terms; the "Last updated" date at the top shows when they last changed.

7. Contact

Questions, lost keys and refunds: support@token-board.app
